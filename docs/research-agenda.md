# Research agenda

## Central question
**Can explicit evidence/dependency state make long administrative workflows easier to audit and unblock?**

## Hypotheses
### H1
Explicit decision ownership reduces ambiguous 'pending' states.

### H2
Required-evidence IDs make readiness criteria more reproducible than free-text notes.

### H3
Audit events enable reconstruction of why a case changed state.

## Experiments
1. Generate cases spanning all state-machine branches and property-test transition invariants.
2. Add duplicate/conflicting evidence in a future version and evaluate provenance-aware resolution.
3. Measure next-action accuracy against manually authored synthetic workflow traces.

## Metrics
- state accuracy
- blocked-reason accuracy
- missing-evidence recall
- audit completeness
- transition invariant violations
